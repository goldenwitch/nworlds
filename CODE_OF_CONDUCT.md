# Code of Conduct

nworlds is a technical project and a shared learning space. Participants are
expected to:

- communicate directly, respectfully, and in good faith;
- focus criticism on code, evidence, and decisions rather than people;
- make room for different levels of familiarity and different perspectives;
- keep private information and credentials out of public project spaces; and
- accept correction when new evidence changes an earlier conclusion.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication
of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `@goldenwitch` through GitHub. Maintainers
may remove content, restrict participation, or close a contribution when that
is necessary to keep the project usable and safe.
